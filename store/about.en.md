## Browse with the controls already in your hands

Move the pointer with the left stick, use the D-pad for small adjustments, and press A to click. The right stick scrolls in all four directions. Use the shoulder triggers to zoom in on text or fit more of the page on screen.

Press X to open the address screen. The on-screen keyboard has shortcuts for common parts of a web address. You can also enter a search or plug in a physical keyboard.

Browser uses WPE WebKit and has one tab. It asks sites for their mobile layout so pages can make better use of the handheld screen. The browsing session is temporary. Media playback and WebRTC are disabled.
